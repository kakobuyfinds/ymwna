02 / From a find to a decision

How to use an OopBuy spreadsheet

01

Build a small shortlist

Start with a category and spending limit. Record the exact item name, size and color rather than saving a photo alone. Export this selection if you want a starting file for your own comparison sheet.

02

Verify the source listing

Open CAFANS and search the item name. Review the original seller link, selected variant and current price. If you plan to use OopBuy, check whether its current ordering flow accepts that source link before paying.

03

Review the full purchase

Compare measurements, available inspection photos, return terms and a destination-specific shipping quote. Keep unanswered questions visible and resolve them before committing to the order or shipment.

03 / Know your numbers

The item price is only the starting point.

Use one currency for every field. Add the charges from your own quotes to create a planning subtotal. This worksheet does not retrieve rates or calculate customs charges.

An empty field is treated as zero for the calculation, but an unknown charge still needs checking. The result is not a checkout quote.

04 / Details worth checking

A practical product comparison checklist

CategoryRecord before orderingReview in item photos
Shoes & slidesSelected size system, foot or insole measurement method, width and exact color.Pair consistency, sole edges, straps, visible damage and any requested measurements.
Hoodies & sweatersChest width, body length, sleeve length and the seller’s stated fabric composition.Seams, cuffs, zipper function where visible, print placement and measurement photos.
T-shirtsShoulder width, chest width, length and intended fit against a shirt you already own.Neckline, print alignment, embroidery, stains and the selected color or variant.
Bags & accessoriesDimensions, strap length, closures, included components and stated materials.Stitching, corners, hardware, attachment points and missing parts.

Use measurements, not just size labels

Compare like-for-like measurements in the same units. A flat garment chest width is different from body circumference. Ask how the seller measures the item, then compare it with a similar garment you own. A familiar letter size alone does not establish fit.

Separate catalog photos from inspection photos

A listing image describes the offered product; it does not document the exact item you will receive. If your purchasing service provides warehouse photos, inspect your selected variant and request missing views before its relevant return deadline. Photos alone cannot establish authenticity or long-term durability.

05 / Quick answers

OopBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OopBuy spreadsheet?

An OopBuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds, source links, prices, categories and comparison notes. This page provides a web-based selection imported from CAFANS, plus filters and a downloadable CSV for your own research.

Where do the product buttons take me?

All shopping links on this page open https://cafans.com. They do not open an OopBuy checkout or automatically add a product to a cart. Search the displayed product name on CAFANS to find its current listing and available purchasing options.

Are these live prices or confirmed stock?

No. The displayed amounts are estimated USD prices captured from CAFANS on November 19, 2026. This is a static catalog selection. Check the source for the latest price, currency conversion, variants and availability.

Can I order every CAFANS item through OopBuy?

Compatibility is not verified here. Review the original marketplace link and check whether OopBuy currently accepts that listing and product type. Do not assume that appearing in a spreadsheet establishes agent support.

Does the listed price include international shipping?

The displayed amount should be treated as an item-price estimate. Obtain separate quotes for domestic delivery, service or payment fees, international shipping and any destination-related charges that apply. Use the budget worksheet to organize the amounts you have checked.

How do I save the spreadsheet?

With JavaScript enabled, choose “Export filtered list as CSV” above the catalog. The file includes the visible product names, categories, estimated USD prices, source destination and catalog review date. Add your own variant, measurement and seller-link columns after opening it in your spreadsheet application.

Is this an official OopBuy website?

No. This is an independent research page and is not operated by or affiliated with OopBuy. It does not take payments, arrange shipping, guarantee products or authenticate listings. Product and brand names identify entries as displayed in the source catalog.
